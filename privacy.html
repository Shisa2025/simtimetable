Privacy

Your timetable choices stay on your device.

This page explains what the site stores locally and how third-party hosting and advertising services may process information.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Information stored in your browser

The site has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, student ID, email address, or timetable. It uses browser local storage for:

  • the latest timetable snapshot loaded by the site or imported by you; and
  • your light, dark, or system theme preference.

This information remains on the device and browser where it was saved. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser data.

Google AdSense and cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to fund its operation. Google and other advertising vendors may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses, or similar identifiers to deliver, personalise, and measure ads. Google may use advertising cookies based on visits to this and other websites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are shown a Google-certified consent message where required. You can consent, decline, or manage available options. You can also review how Google uses information from partner sites and manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.

Hosting and public data services

The website is hosted by Vercel, the source and daily schedule snapshot are hosted on GitHub, and schedule data originates from SIM’s public scheduling service. These providers may process standard request information such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and timestamps under their own privacy policies and operational requirements.

Timetable data

The published timetable describes rooms and scheduled activities. Do not import or paste files containing personal or confidential information. Standalone HTML exports are created locally in your browser and are not uploaded by this application.

Your choices

  • Use the consent message to manage advertising choices where it is available.
  • Use browser settings to block or delete cookies and local storage.
  • Use Google Ads Settings to control personalised advertising associated with Google.

Questions

For privacy questions or corrections, open an issue in the project’s public GitHub repository. Do not include personal information in a public issue.
