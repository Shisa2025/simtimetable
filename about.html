About this project

A clearer way to find student-accessible rooms.

SIM Timetable turns the public campus schedule into a practical room finder for students.

Independent student tool. This website is not affiliated with, operated by, or endorsed by Singapore Institute of Management (SIM).

What Free Access means

A room appears in the finder only when SIM’s schedule contains an explicit Free Access activity for that room and time. This is the strongest available indication that students may use it.

A room with no booking is not treated as available. It may be locked, reserved, or otherwise inaccessible. Unallocated gaps are labelled “Unknown” for the same reason.

Where the schedule comes from

The data comes from SIM’s public campus scheduling service. An automated browser refreshes the published snapshot shortly after midnight Singapore time. Every page shows the schedule date and how recently it was collected.

Limitations

  • Room access can change without notice, and the physical room sign or instructions from SIM always take priority.
  • The daily snapshot may become stale if the source or automated refresh is unavailable.
  • Capacity is extracted from SIM’s room description where available and may not describe the current seating arrangement.

Open project

The site is a small, dependency-free static project. You can report inaccurate data, accessibility problems, or usability ideas through GitHub Issues.
